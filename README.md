# Brand Dream Team

Your 8 AI employees, your Dream Team HQ, and a Studio that batch edits your reels in your brand. By Destiny Alexis.

## Install (once, about 1 minute)

Brand Dream Team needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher).

1. In Claude on the web or the desktop app, open **Customize → Plugins**.
2. Tap **+** → **Add marketplace** and paste: `Brand-dream-team/-bdt-6s8d32jtgf-zac5qjuly1-clq9cfilln-4rc5xqimis-vl2kmnnhka`
3. Tap **Install** on **Brand Dream Team**.
4. Start a new chat and say: **Set up my Dream Team**

## Updates

New versions arrive on their own. To get one right away, say **Upgrade me** in a new chat. Nothing to delete or re-upload.
