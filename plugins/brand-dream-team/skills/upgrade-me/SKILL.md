# Upgrade me

Current version: **1.0.7** (the `version` in this plugin's `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`).

The creator never deletes files or re-uploads anything. Keep every step short and friendly.

## 1. Make sure they have the newest plugin

New versions arrive on their own. If they've just been told about an update and don't see it yet, tell them:
*Customize → Plugins → Brand Dream Team → Update* (if there's no Update button, they're already current). Then start a new chat and say "Upgrade me" again.

## 2. Upgrade their HQ

Follow the **Update my HQ** row in the brand-dream-team-setup skill: read their current Dream Team HQ, keep its `CONFIG` (team names, Brand DNA, colors, fonts, tasks, hooks), rebuild it from today's template, and publish it as a **new version of the same artifact**, never a new one. Then tell them to refresh their HQ.

If you can't find their HQ, ask them to paste their HQ link.

If saving over their HQ is refused (for example "network access is off" or "can't change an artifact from another chat"), don't make a second HQ. Tell them, in one short message:
1. Open their HQ (give them the link) and start a chat **from the HQ page**, then say "Upgrade me" there.
2. Turn on **Settings → Capabilities → Create and edit files → Use the internet while making files**, then start a new chat. The video editor needs this too, to download its tools the first time.

## 3. The Studio

Nothing to do. The Studio is one shared page that's always the newest version. Their personal Studio link keeps their colors.

## 4. Tell them what's new

One short message: their version number, what's new (from the list below, newest first), and one thing to try.
